Who We Are

We are a CRO built for one mission-critical area: pediatric rare disease clinical research. Our cross-functional project teams are configured around each study's scientific, ethical, and regulatory demands.

Unlike general-purpose CROs, we apply pediatric-first statistical strategy for small populations, limited prior evidence, and high-stakes regulatory decisions. Our model prioritizes agility, transparency, and sponsor collaboration from study design through submission.

Mission, Vision, and Values

Mission

We accelerate the development of treatments for pediatric rare diseases by delivering rigorous, ethical, and regulatory-ready statistical evidence.

Vision

To lead a transformation in pediatric rare disease research by setting the standard for transparent, credible, and trustworthy clinical evidence.

Values

  • Children First: We center pediatric clinical, ethical, and regulatory needs in every decision.
  • Rigor with Reality: We combine methodological strength with practical feasibility in rare disease trials.
  • Transparent Evidence: We use reproducible methods and communicate uncertainty clearly.

Services

Specialized solutions for Phase I-III pediatric rare disease development programs.

Clinical Trial Design

Protocol-level design strategy, sample size justification, randomization planning, and adaptive design support for small-population studies.

Biostatistical Analysis

Interim and final analyses using fit-for-purpose frequentist and Bayesian approaches for complex pediatric and rare disease endpoints.

Data Science & Programming

Analysis-ready dataset preparation, reproducible code pipelines, and high-quality tables, listings, and figures for sponsor and regulatory deliverables.

Regulatory Evidence Support

Statistical documentation and response support for IND, NDA, and BLA submissions aligned with FDA and EMA expectations.

Senior Biostatistician - Pediatric Rare Disease Trials

We are seeking a Senior Biostatistician to join our Scientific Division and lead statistical planning, analysis, and reporting for Phase I-III pediatric rare disease clinical trials.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design clinical trials for rare disease indications with small patient populations, including sample size justification, randomization strategies, and adaptive designs.
  • Develop Statistical Analysis Plans (SAPs) in collaboration with clinical teams, data managers, and regulatory specialists.
  • Conduct interim and final statistical analyses using frequentist and Bayesian methods appropriate for rare disease settings.
  • Prepare statistical sections of regulatory submissions (IND, NDA, BLA) and respond to FDA/EMA queries.
  • Mentor junior biostatisticians and statistical programmers on trial methodology and regulatory standards.

Required Qualifications

  • PhD or Master's degree in Biostatistics, Statistics, or related quantitative field.
  • Strong proficiency in SAS and R for statistical programming and analysis.
  • Experience with adaptive designs, Bayesian methods, or other advanced statistical approaches for small populations.
  • Demonstrated ability to develop Statistical Analysis Plans and interpret complex trial data.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills for technical and non-technical audiences.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience specifically with pediatric clinical trials or rare disease programs.
  • Publication record in peer-reviewed biostatistics or clinical research journals.
  • Knowledge of simulation methods for trial design optimization.

Compensation

Base salary range: $155,000-$210,000, plus annual performance bonus eligibility and comprehensive benefits including health insurance, dental and vision coverage, 401(k) with employer matching, professional development funding, and generous PTO.

How This Role Contributes to Our Mission and Goals

This role directly advances our mission to accelerate treatment development for pediatric rare diseases by ensuring that clinical trial designs and analyses are scientifically rigorous, ethically grounded, and regulatory-ready. The Senior Biostatistician strengthens trial quality, improves decision confidence for sponsors, and helps bring credible evidence to regulators and families faster.

Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis until April 30, 2026.

Data Scientist - AI & Machine Learning for Rare Disease Research

We are seeking a Data Scientist with expertise in artificial intelligence and machine learning to support innovative analytical approaches for pediatric rare disease clinical research.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and validate machine learning models for rare disease patient identification, outcome prediction, and safety signal detection.
  • Apply NLP to extract structured data from EHRs, medical literature, trial documents, and patient registries.
  • Integrate and analyze heterogeneous data sources including clinical trial data, real-world data, genomic data, and patient-reported outcomes.
  • Build predictive models to support trial feasibility, patient enrollment forecasting, endpoint selection, and treatment response prediction.
  • Create reproducible analysis pipelines and dashboards for sponsor reporting and regulatory submissions.

Required Qualifications

  • Master's degree or PhD in Data Science, Computer Science, Biostatistics, Computational Biology, Bioinformatics, or related quantitative field.
  • Strong programming skills in Python (scikit-learn, TensorFlow/PyTorch, pandas, numpy) and/or R.
  • Experience with supervised and unsupervised learning methods.
  • Knowledge of clinical data standards (OMOP, CDISC, FHIR) and healthcare data types.
  • Strong communication skills to explain technical methods to clinical, statistical, and regulatory audiences.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with rare disease data, small sample methodologies, or pediatric research.
  • Knowledge of causal inference methods, propensity score matching, inverse probability weighting, or synthetic control approaches.
  • Understanding of regulatory requirements for algorithm validation, transparency, and bias assessment.
  • Knowledge of federated learning or privacy-preserving machine learning techniques.
  • Experience with Bayesian machine learning or probabilistic programming (PyMC, Stan).

Compensation

Base salary range: $140,000-$195,000, plus annual performance bonus eligibility and comprehensive benefits including health insurance, dental and vision coverage, 401(k) with employer matching, professional development funding, and generous PTO.

How This Role Contributes to Our Mission and Goals

This role supports our mission by applying advanced analytics and machine learning to improve trial feasibility, patient identification, and evidence generation in pediatric rare disease research. The Data Scientist helps transform complex data into transparent, actionable insights that improve study execution and strengthen sponsor and regulatory decision-making.
